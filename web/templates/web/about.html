From one food truck to a whole kitchen

Roundbelly's started in 2020 as a pizza truck in South Bend, moving around town with a menu that changed as often as the parking spot. People kept asking us to bring it to their parties, and then to their fundraisers.

Today the truck is the Bus, a red Blue Bird school bus with a wood-burning pizza oven inside, and it caters graduations, weddings and company events across Northern Indiana and Southern Michigan. Behind it is our own USDA-inspected kitchen, where we make the frozen pizzas that schools, teams and churches sell to raise money, and that local stores keep in their freezers.

Same recipes, same crew, whether it's coming off the Bus hot or out of your oven at home.
