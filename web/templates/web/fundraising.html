How it works

  1. Apply

    Tell us about your group below. We'll call to set your sale dates and delivery spot.

  2. Sell for two weeks

    Every seller gets a flyer with an order form. Buyers pay the seller by cash or check, or order online by card.

  3. We make the pizzas

    When the sale closes, we make every pizza fresh in our USDA-inspected kitchen. That takes one to two weeks, depending on how many you sold.

  4. We deliver, sorted by seller

    One drop-off at your school or meeting spot, boxed by seller so each kid can hand out their own orders.

  5. Settle up at delivery

    We total it up and bring a statement. Online orders are already credited to you, less the 3% card fee. If we owe you, the check comes with the pizzas.
